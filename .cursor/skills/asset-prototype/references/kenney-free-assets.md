## Search hints

- Platformer, farm, factory, or sketch art → Tiny Factory, Scribble Platformer Expansion, or Tiny Farm.
- City, street, building, or industrial environment → City Kit (Industrial) or City Kit (Roads).
- Dungeon or cave → Mini Dungeon or Modular Cave Kit.
- Controller, keyboard, crosshair, board game, or flag UI → Input Prompts, Crosshair Pack, Domino
  Pack, or Flag Pack.
- Repeating materials, masks, or lighting textures → Pattern Pack Extra or Light Masks.
