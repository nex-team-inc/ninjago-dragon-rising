# Quaternius free asset catalog

Use this catalog to choose a free Quaternius pack before searching for a specific asset. It is a
snapshot checked against the [Quaternius free-assets site](https://quaternius.com/index.html) on
2026-09-22. The linked pack pages explicitly describe their free downloads as CC0 and usable in
personal, educational, and commercial projects.

## Current homepage categories

The homepage currently groups its catalog under these useful search categories:

- Fast Food Consumables
- Nature Trees and Fantasy
- Vehicles, Cars, and Transport
- Nature and Trees

Use the [Quaternius homepage](https://quaternius.com/) to browse those categories. The pack index
below provides direct links for the larger free packs that are useful for prototyping.

## Characters and animation

- [Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) — humanoid
  base characters with multiple proportions and interchangeable hairstyles; FBX and glTF.
- [Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy](https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html)
  — modular fantasy outfits designed to work with the Universal Base Characters; FBX and glTF.
- [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) —
  humanoid locomotion, combat, gun, and emote animations.
- [Universal Animation Library 2](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html) —
  humanoid melee, parkour, farming, fishing, and zombie animations.

## Environments and props

- [Downtown City MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/downtowncitymegakit.html) — modular city
  blocks, buildings, and street pieces.
- [Medieval Village MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalvillagemegakit.html) — grid-based
  medieval walls, floors, roofs, stairs, doors, and windows.
- [Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html) — grid-based
  science-fiction floors, walls, doors, columns, aliens, and props.
- [Sci-Fi Essentials Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/scifiessentialskit.html) — futuristic
  enemies, weapons, screens, crates, and props.
- [Fantasy Props MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html) — low-poly
  medieval furniture, tools, weapons, books, potions, food, and breakables.
- [Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html) — stylized
  trees, plants, flowers, rocks, grass, and bushes.

## Search hints

- Character, humanoid, NPC, or outfit → Universal Base Characters and Modular Character Outfits -
  Fantasy.
- Walk, run, combat, parkour, farming, or emote → Universal Animation Library or Universal
  Animation Library 2.
- City, street, or urban environment → Downtown City MegaKit.
- Medieval village or modular building → Medieval Village MegaKit.
- Sci-fi corridor, spaceship, robot, or futuristic prop → Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit or Sci-Fi
  Essentials Kit.
- Fantasy furniture, weapon, potion, or market prop → Fantasy Props MegaKit.
- Tree, plant, rock, or natural environment → Stylized Nature MegaKit or the homepage's Nature
  categories.
